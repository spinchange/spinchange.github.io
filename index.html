spinchange

Old books made readable again, reading notes, and a few small tools. Most of it built together with AI agents and published from GitHub.

Original work

  • EtymologiaeIsidore of Seville's seventh-century encyclopedia taken as a frame for something new: twenty books and 218 chapters of original verse, written by guilds of AI models and linked chapter to chapter across the whole work.

Translations

  • The Odysseytheclaudyssey.comA complete line-for-line English translation from the Greek, with annotations, a name index with pronunciations, and EPUB, Kindle and PDF editions.
  • The Iliadwrath-sing-goddess.comA complete line-for-line English translation from the Greek, with annotations and a translator's commentary on every book.
  • The Histories of PolybiusBooks 1 to 39, the whole extant corpus, in modern English from the Greek, with EPUB and PDF editions.
  • The Odyssey in English hexameterA second Odyssey, this one in dactylic hexameter, line for line.
  • De Divina ProportioneEnglish translation of Pacioli's 1498 treatise with Leonardo da Vinci's sixty plates.

Reading editions

  • Great Books of the WorldA world-literature library in public-domain English translations, with a catalog site and hand-built ePubs.
  • The Decline and Fall of the Roman EmpireGibbon, in an illustrated HTML edition.
  • Extraordinary Popular Delusions and the Madness of CrowdsCharles Mackay, 1852, as a multi-page HTML edition.
  • The Federalist PapersAll 85 essays as a linked vault.
  • As We May ThinkVannevar Bush's 1945 essay in a distraction-free reading edition.
  • Computing Machinery and IntelligenceAlan Turing's 1950 paper in a distraction-free reading edition.
  • A Mathematical Theory of CommunicationClaude Shannon's 1948 paper in a distraction-free reading edition.
